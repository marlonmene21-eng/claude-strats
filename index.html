Strat Buttons

Chart tools for TopstepX and Vest. Install takes about a minute — no downloads.

Install

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: press Ctrl+Shift+B (Mac: Cmd+Shift+B).
  2. Drag each button below up onto the bookmarks bar (or into a folder). Don't click them on this page.
  3. Open your chart on topstepx.com or Vest and click a bookmark. A panel opens and the chart gets marked up. Click it again (or ✕) to close.
  4. NQ gamma levels (AMD Range Model): click GEX Update on your TopstepX tab, then click GEX Update again in the CBOE tab that opens. Do this once a day. BTC levels update on their own.
PO3 Watch

Live PO3 frames (NY AM + 7–11 ET hourly): sweep → CISD → FVG entry, stop and two targets.

Scriv Model

Killzone raid → 5m CISD → OTE entry (RB/FVG), STDV targets.

ICT Killzones

Scanner: killzone boxes, session highs/lows, FVGs, RBs, CISDs (tapped and untapped).

AMD Range Model

Market-structure bias, multi-timeframe RBs/IFVGs, GEX levels, and one graded trade plan at a time.

GEX Update

Refreshes NQ options-gamma levels for the AMD Range Model (see step 4).

Good to know

  • Updates are automatic — every click loads the latest version.
  • Read-only: the tools only read chart data and draw on the chart. They never place orders or touch your account.
  • Panels refresh every 30 seconds and can be dragged anywhere.
  • If Chrome blocks the CBOE pop-up, choose “Always allow pop-ups” for topstepx.com.
